Eight pillars of on-chain control

Every feature is engineered for a single goal: give you total visibility and absolute custody of your TestNet assets.

Multi-Wallet Vault

Generate unlimited TestNet addresses with AES-256 encrypted private keys, never exposed beyond the signing module.

Live Transaction Stream

Kafka-driven event pipeline tracks every tx from PENDING to CONFIRMED with sub-second updates and real-time status.

Anomaly Detection

Five behavioral rules score every transfer in real time: unusual amount, new destination, rapid fire, off-hours, round amount.
